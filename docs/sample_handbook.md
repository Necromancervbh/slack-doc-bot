# Employee Handbook

## Welcome to the Team!

We are thrilled to have you join our team. This handbook covers everything you need to know to get started and thrive at our company.

## Time Off and Vacation Policy

All full-time employees receive **20 days of paid vacation** per year. Vacation days accrue at a rate of 1.67 days per month.

- Vacation requests must be submitted at least **2 weeks in advance** via the HR portal.
- Unused vacation days carry over up to a maximum of **10 days** per year.
- Sick days are separate and unlimited (with manager notification).

## Work Hours

Our core hours are **10 AM to 4 PM** in your local timezone. Outside of these hours, flexible scheduling is supported.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**. Full-remote arrangements require manager and HR approval.

## Code of Conduct

We expect all employees to treat colleagues with respect and professionalism. Harassment or discrimination of any kind will not be tolerated.

## Benefits

- Health, dental, and vision insurance
- 401(k) with 4% company match
- $1,000/year learning and development budget
- Free lunch on Fridays
- Home office stipend: $500 one-time
